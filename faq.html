Frequently asked questions

Common questions about setting up and running Avantis Hub.

The basics

What is Avantis Hub for?

It lets things that send cues (MIDI, OSC, Ableton Live, a phone) control an Allen & Heath Avantis: mutes, faders, scene recalls and groups of changes at once. See the manual.

Does it work with other consoles, such as dLive, SQ or Qu?

No. It is written for the Avantis and its control protocol. Other consoles use different messages and channel counts, and the hub does not guess.

Does anything need to be installed on the console?

No. The hub uses the control port the Avantis already has. You only need its IP address and its MIDI channel setting.

Do I need a MIDI interface or cable?

No. The hub talks to the console over the network. MIDI from apps on the Mac reaches the hub through a software port called "Avantis Hub". If you do have a controller or interface, the hub can listen to it directly.

Can I use it with no MIDI or OSC at all?

Yes. Quick buttons, their keyboard shortcuts, the menu bar panel and the phone app need nothing else.

Which Macs and devices does it run on?

A Mac with macOS 15 or later. The companion needs iOS or iPadOS 18 or later.

Does it need the internet?

Only to check for updates. Everything else happens on your local network.

Connecting to the console

The hub will not connect.
  • Check the Mac and console are on the same network and the IP address is right. Click Find beside Console IP to list the consoles that answer, or read the address on the console under Utility / Control / Network.
  • Check macOS allows it: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network, with Avantis Hub switched on.
  • If the venue network is managed, ask whether devices are allowed to reach each other.
Find does not list my console.

Find only reaches consoles on the same network as the Mac. A console on another network, reached through a router, does not answer and has to be typed in. Check the Local Network permission as well.

A banner says my console answered at another address.

The board would not connect, so the hub looked once and found one console that could be yours somewhere else. This happens when the network hands the console a new address. Check the name, then click Use This Address, or Not Now to leave things alone. The hub never changes the address by itself, and it offers nothing if two consoles answer.

It connects, but no channel names appear.

The Base MIDI channel does not match the console. Set it to the same value as Utility / Control / MIDI on the console. The console's default is 12 to 16.

If the channel is right, something else may already be using the console's MIDI control port. The console answers one such connection at a time, so a second one connects and hears nothing. Close the other app or the other hub and reconnect.

Can I use Avantis Director or the tablet apps at the same time?

Yes. They use a different connection to the console, and the hub stays connected alongside them.

Which firmware does the console need?

Any. Set the board's Firmware to match: "V2.0 and later" or "Before V2.0". Firmware V2.0 added inputs 65 to 96, more groups, auxes and matrices, and UFX. On "Before V2.0" the hub leaves those out and never sends to them.

Why do only some of my mixes have names?

The console only answers for the channels and mixes it is set up with. A group or aux your console does not use keeps its plain number.

The list of channels is very long. Can I see just mine?

Turn on Named, in the Board Targets column or at the top of Show view. It hides every channel nobody has named on the console. In Show view you can also build your own pages: see the manual.

Why can't I change the IP address?

It is locked while connected. Click Disconnect first.

Some faders show "?" or look dimmed.

The console reports a fader or mute when it changes, and its MIDI control cannot be asked where everything is. A dimmed value is remembered from an earlier connection and may be out of date. A "?" has never been seen. Move the control once and the hub shows the live value. See the manual.

What does "MUTE ?" mean?

The console has not said whether that channel is muted since the hub connected. Pressing it mutes. Once the mute changes, by the hub or at the console, the button shows the real state.

Can the hub read the mutes when it connects?

For inputs, as an experiment. Each board has an option, "Read input mutes on connecting", that is off unless you turn it on. With it on, the hub makes one short read-only visit on the connection Avantis Director uses and fills in which inputs are muted. It was worked out by watching Director, not from Allen & Heath's documents, so try it at a quiet console first. Faders, DCAs and mixes are not read.

Why does "turn up" from my phone say it needs the fader's level?

Moving a fader "up by 3 dB" needs a known starting point. If the console has not reported that fader since the hub connected, the hub refuses instead of guessing. Move the fader once, or set it to a level instead.

Why doesn't the hub know the current scene?

The console only says which scene is current when one is recalled. Until then the hub shows "Not known yet".

Can the hub show scene names?

No. The console's control protocol lets the hub recall a scene by number but not read the list of names.

The connection dropped in the middle of a service. What happens?

The hub keeps trying to reconnect and carries on when the console is back. Nothing is sent while it is away, and cues that arrive in the gap are ignored, not saved up.

Can two hubs control one console?

No. The console answers one connection on its MIDI control port at a time, so the second hub would connect and hear nothing. For one setup on two Macs, share the setup and run one hub at a time.

Armed, Disarmed and Panic

I sent a cue and nothing happened on the console.

Check the hub is Armed and the console is connected, then read the Monitor. A BLOCK line gives the reason, for example "Ignored, hub is disarmed".

What exactly does Panic do?

It disarms the hub and stops any glide where it is. It sends nothing to the console: no mutes, no fader moves, no scene.

Does Panic mute everything?

No, on purpose. Muting a whole room is a decision for the person at the console. If you want a "mute everything" control, build a quick button for it.

Can a MIDI note, OSC message or phone arm the hub?

No. Arming is done at the Mac. The one exception is the setting you choose yourself, Arm when the console connects.

The Armed button says Armed but is not glowing.

The hub is armed but has no console. It glows only when it can actually send. The menu bar icon is red in the same state.

If I trigger a toggle while disarmed, will it be out of step when I arm?

No. A trigger that cannot be sent is ignored completely and the toggle does not flip its memory.

Can the hub get into a feedback loop with the console?

It is built not to. The hub remembers each change it has just made. If the console reports that same change back, the hub recognises it as its own and does not act on it again.

Could a burst of cues flood the console?

The hub spaces out what it sends, so a burst is delivered in order at a pace the console handles.

Mappings and triggers

Nothing shows in the Triggers column.
  • MIDI: the sending app must use the port called "Avantis Hub" as its output. A hardware controller must be switched on under MIDI in the Inputs settings.
  • OSC: Listen for OSC must be on, and the sender must use the Mac's address and the hub's port (9300 unless changed).
Can a hardware controller or another computer send cues?

Yes. In the Inputs settings, the MIDI tab lists every MIDI input on the Mac. Move a control, see which input's dot lights, and switch that one on. A network MIDI session from another computer or an iPad shows up the same way.

Every cue is arriving twice.

An input is switched on that an app is already passing on to the Avantis Hub port, so the hub hears the same note by two routes. Switch that input off, or tie the mapping to one of the two with From in the inspector.

Can the same note do different things from different controllers?

Yes. In a mapping's inspector, or a quick button's editor, From ties the trigger to one input: the Avantis Hub port, one of the inputs you switched on, or the console. Left on Any input, it fires from all of them.

Can a SoftKey on the console fire a cue?

Yes, if the console passes its SoftKey MIDI over the network. Turn on "Use the console's SoftKeys and MIDI strips as triggers" in the board's Options, and set the SoftKey on the console to send a note on a MIDI channel outside the console's own five. See Sending cues.

My note shows up as C3 but my other software calls it C4.

The hub names notes the way Ableton Live and Logic do, where middle C (note 60) is C3. Some software calls the same note C4. The note number is what matters, and it is the same.

What is the difference between Toggle and Set?

Toggle flips each time, so its result depends on where things were. Set always ends at the value you chose. Use Set for cues that must be right however they are reached.

Can one trigger control several things?

Yes. Add more targets to the mapping, or have it press a quick button.

Can several triggers control the same thing?

Yes. Switch the list to By target to see everything that controls a channel.

What does the warning triangle on a mapping mean?

Another mapping uses the same trigger on the same target. Both fire every time, so two toggles would cancel out. Change one trigger or switch one mapping off.

Can I turn a mapping off without deleting it?

Yes, with the switch at the right of its row.

I closed the inspector and lost my changes.

Cancel, the close button and Esc all back out. Done keeps the changes. Reopen the mapping, make the change again and click Done. For a bigger loss, use Restore from Backup in the Profile menu.

Can I map a hardware fader to a console fader?

Yes, with a Value mapping. Set Range to the levels the fader should cover. If the fader is on a controller, switch its input on in the Inputs settings first.

Can a mapping fade a level over time?

Yes. Set the level change to Glide and give it a time. See Snap and Glide.

Can I control EQ, compressors, gain or pan?

No. The hub offers what the console's control protocol exposes: mutes, faders, sends, assignments and scene recall. On firmware V2.0 and later it also offers the key and scale for the UFX pitch effects, and any UFX parameter the console has been set up to take by MIDI. For anything else, store it in a scene on the console and have the hub recall the scene.

Can the hub set the key for a vocal tuner, or switch it on and off?

On firmware V2.0 and later, yes. Board Targets has a UFX group with the twelve keys and three scales, which set the console's global key and scale. Under Parameters it has named entries, such as Tuner bypass, for parameters you have given a MIDI channel and controller number on the console. A cue at the top of a song can set the key and scale and take the tuner out of bypass together. See What can be controlled.

My UFX cue says "UFX cues are switched off for this board".

Each board has one switch for every UFX key, scale and parameter cue, and it starts off. Open the board's settings, choose Options, and turn on "Send UFX key, scale and parameters". Turn it off again to hold them all back at once.

How do I start a mapping from the thing I want to control?

With no mapping selected, click the target in Board Targets. A new mapping opens for it, ready for its trigger.

Quick buttons

What is the difference between a mapping and a quick button?

A mapping ties one trigger to targets. A quick button is a named set of actions that can be pressed many ways: a click, a keyboard shortcut, MIDI, OSC, a phone, the menu bar or a clip in Live.

Why does my button need two presses from the screen but one from MIDI?

"Require confirm" is there to stop a slip of the hand. Whatever sends MIDI or OSC cannot press twice, so those fire on the first.

I renamed a button. Do my OSC cues still work?

Yes. The OSC address is fixed when the button is first saved and does not change with the name.

I renamed a button. Does its Ableton clip still work?

No. Clips find buttons by their current name, so rename the clip to match.

Can a button run other buttons?

Yes, with "Runs these buttons first". It runs what they do at the moment of the press, so editing one changes every button that runs it.

Two actions in one button set the same fader. Which wins?

The later one.

Can I reorder buttons?

On the Mac, click Edit and drag. On a phone or iPad, tap the arrows and drag. The device's order is its own.

Ableton Live

What do I need for the Ableton link?

AbletonOSC, a free control surface script, installed and chosen in Live's Control Surface settings. Then turn on "Read song information from Ableton Live" in the hub. It needs Live 11 or later.

Where do I get AbletonOSC?

From its page on GitHub. Click Code, then Download ZIP. Unzip it, rename the folder to AbletonOSC, move it into Music/Ableton/User Library/Remote Scripts in your home folder, and restart Live. There are other projects with similar names, and the hub only works with this one. The manual has the steps in full.

My clip does not press its button.
  • The track's name must start with "Hub".
  • The clip's name must match the button's name. Capitals do not matter.
  • The hub must be armed and connected.
  • After a rename, wait a few seconds.
  • Check the Monitor. It names any clip that matched no button.
Will launching a scene in Live press my buttons?

Yes, for each clip in that row that sits on a Hub track.

The hub says another app is using Ableton's reply port.

AbletonOSC answers on one fixed port, and only one app on a Mac can hold it. Close the other app, or use it to forward what the hub needs. The hub takes over by itself when the port comes free.

Can the hub start or stop Live, or launch clips?

No. It only reads from Live.

Can I use MIDI clips in Live instead?

Yes. Send notes from a MIDI track to the "Avantis Hub" port and map them. That needs no AbletonOSC.

iPhone and iPad

The device cannot find the Mac.

Both must be on the same network and the hub must be running. Guest networks and some venue networks stop devices seeing each other. The device also needs permission to use the local network, in its Settings under Avantis Hub.

The pairing code is refused.

A code works for one device, runs out after a few minutes and is cancelled after five wrong tries. Click Pair a Device again for a new one.

A paired device has stopped working.

If it was revoked on the Mac, it must pair again. Otherwise check the network and pull down to refresh.

The buttons on the device are dimmed.

The hub is disarmed, has no console, or the link is down. The banner at the top says which.

Can a phone arm the hub?

No. It can disarm it with Panic, if the Mac allows that device to.

Can a phone change anything on the console?

Only through the hub and only while the hub is armed. By default it can press the quick buttons you allow it. Direct control from Shortcuts is off until you turn it on for that device, and is held to the limits you set.

A fader change from my phone was refused.

It was above the highest level allowed, or a bigger move than allowed. The hub refuses instead of trimming, so a mistake never lands "as close as permitted". The limits are in Settings > Devices on the Mac.

Can I hide some buttons from a volunteer's phone?

Yes. In that device's settings on the Mac, choose "Sees only chosen buttons" and tick the ones to show.

Can one phone work with more than one Mac?

Yes. Use Pair another Mac on the Status screen, then tap a hub to switch.

Does the phone talk to the console?

Never. It talks only to the hub.

Does it work away from the building?

No. The device must be on the same network as the Mac.

Can I say "Hey Siri, mute Pastor"?

The actions are there in Shortcuts and work when run from the Shortcuts app, a widget or a button. Triggering them by voice alone is not yet reliable in this version.

Profiles, backups and sharing

How do I save?

You do not need to. Every change is saved as you make it.

I broke my profile. Can I go back?

Yes. Profile menu > Restore from Backup lists the last 30 dated versions.

I deleted a profile by mistake.

Profile menu > Restore Deleted Profile brings it back.

How do I move my setup to another Mac?

For a one-off, Export to File and Import from File in the Profile menu. To keep two Macs in step, share a folder under Settings > Sharing.

Someone changed the profile on another Mac while I was running a service.

While your hub is armed the change waits, and a banner offers to reload it when you are ready.

A profile called "(conflicted copy)" has appeared.

Two Macs edited the same profile while out of touch. The hub kept both so nothing was lost. Compare them and delete the one you do not want.

I moved a channel on the console and my mappings followed it. Is that right?

Yes. Mappings follow a channel by its name. A banner tells you what was followed.

A banner says strip names have changed and my cues are on hold.

A name the profile relies on has gone, or two channels now share it. The hub will not guess. Choose in the banner where the mappings should go. See the manual.

Updates and the app itself

There is an "Update" marker at the top of the window.

A new version was found while the hub was armed. The hub does not open a window over a service, so it shows the marker and waits. Click it to see what is new.

Can an update restart the hub in the middle of a service?

No. Nothing installs without a click, and an armed hub asks before it quits.

Will an update change my mappings?

No. Boards, profiles, quick buttons and paired devices are kept apart from the app.

I closed the window. Is the hub still running?

Yes. It lives in the menu bar and keeps working. Click the menu bar icon, or its Dock icon, to open the window again. To stop it, choose Quit.

I opened the app again and nothing happened.

Only one hub runs at a time. Opening it again brings the running one forward. An armed hub is never replaced by a second copy.

What does the colour of the menu bar icon mean?

Cyan is armed and connected. Red means the console is lost, or the hub is armed with no console. Plain is everything else.

How do I send you what went wrong?

Click Copy in the Monitor, then paste that into your message along with the version number from Settings > Updates.
